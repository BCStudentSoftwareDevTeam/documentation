## Color

Berea is using a palette primarily utilizing blue and white. However, there are additional accent colors that may be used sparingly to support design.

The primary color types are blue (Berea Blue) and white, the accent colors are orange, teal, light blue, green, and yellow (#FEB047).

## Usage Guidelines

### Logo Usage
- Use the internal logo for application interfaces
- Use the external logo for public-facing materials
- Maintain proper spacing and proportions
- Do not alter logo colors or typography

### Color Usage
- **Primary colors** should dominate the design
- **Accent colors** should be used sparingly for highlights and emphasis
- Maintain sufficient contrast for accessibility
- Test color combinations across different devices and screens

### Typography
- **Verdana** should be used on side bars and headers
- **Proxima Nova** can be used as an alternative sans serif
- **Times New Roman** is the preferred font for digital applications
- Maintain consistent font hierarchy throughout applications
